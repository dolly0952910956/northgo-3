About Northgo

Northgo was founded in Taipei in 1988 as Max Seair Logistics Inc. On 11 November 2024 the company took the name Northgo. The team and the work stayed.

Vision

To be the most trusted name in logistics. Trust is the test for each job: the client should be able to come back with the next problem.

Mission

Making logistics effortless. Remove the steps that do not help the shipment, and leave a move the client can follow.

Values

Northgo states the standard as doing the right thing, not only doing things right. Everyone at the company is responsible for these four.

Customer-centric approach

Decisions and services are judged by the value they create for the client.

Integrity and transparency

Clients are told what is happening, in plain terms.

Innovation and excellence

The service changes when a better way to move the cargo is clear.

Sustainability and responsibility

Look for ways to reduce the environmental impact of the work and to stay responsible to the communities around the offices.

Our story

The company started in Taipei in 1988 to do logistics with more care than a simple handoff. It grew as Max Seair Logistics Inc.

In 2024 the name changed so the brand matched the direction of the work. Northgo is the name from 11 November 2024. The people doing the work did not change.

What has changed?

The name. Northgo is the brand used with clients from that date.

What remains the same?

The team, the standard of work, and the commitment to the client built over more than thirty years.
