Freight Logistics

End-to-end forwarding by sea, air, road, and rail, with customs support for bulk, delicate, and time-critical cargo.

How a shipment is handled

Northgo plans the route, books the move, and follows the cargo through to delivery. Specialists at origin and destination adjust the plan for cost, routing, or transit time.

Ocean Freight

Import and export sea freight, with sailings on carriers Northgo contracts directly.

  • FCL: full-container shipping with access to major lanes.
  • LCL: consolidated shipping across Northgo’s customer volume.
  • Origin and destination teams for cost, route, or transit time.

Air Freight

Air freight when transit time matters, with visibility from pickup through arrival and routing built around the shipment.

Land Transport

Trucking after cargo leaves the ship or aircraft. Partners get the shipment details they need to deliver on time, with tracking along the way.

Customs Clearance

Before import, the Harmonized System code drives duties and taxes. Northgo’s customs team checks that classification so the goods can clear.
