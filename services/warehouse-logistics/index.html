Warehouse Logistics

Storage that sits inside the wider move, including facilities at Taichung Port for project and offshore cargo.

Storage tied to the shipment

The warehouses are not a separate product from the freight. They hold cargo where the routes already run, especially for offshore and large projects.

Located warehouses

Facilities at Taichung Port sit near shipping routes and roads, so cargo spends less time between the ship and the warehouse.

Flexible storage

General cargo, temperature-controlled goods, and secure space for high-value or hazardous materials.

Inventory management

A warehouse management system shows stock as it changes, so orders can be picked against what is actually on hand.

Value-added services

Work done at the warehouse before the cargo moves again.

  • Pick and pack, kitting, and labeling.
  • Inspection and repackaging.
  • Container leasing and sales, including DNV-certified containers for offshore and industrial use.

Offshore logistics

Taichung Port space is used for offshore equipment and components, including offshore wind and other maritime projects. Northgo stores, handles, and forwards that cargo onward.
