Shipping Agency

Port agency and marine services, from berthing arrangements to crew matters.

Types of vesselsPort agency and marine services

A vessel call, handled in advance

Northgo works with port authorities and the other parties on the call so the ship can berth, work cargo, and sail with less idle time. The agency brief covers documents, crew, and the owner’s interests while the ship is in port.

Types of vessels

The agency desk handles these vessel groups.

Dry bulk

Agriculture products, cement, clinker, clay, coal, fertilizer, grain, ore, salt, and steel.

Tankers

Liquefied chemicals, ammonia, crude, CPP, LNG, LPG, lubricant, molasses, palm oil, and vegetable oil.

Break bulk

Conventional general cargo ships, project cargo ships, and heavy-lift carriers.

Cruise

Fishery training boats, yachts, and passenger ships.

Offshore

Dredgers, cable ships, jack-up vessels, crew transit vessels, heavy-lift ships, supply boats, working barges, and salvage tugs.

Specialised carriers

Heavy-lift carriers, submersible ships, and cable-laying barges.

Container ships

Panamax container ships, ultra-large container vessels, and feeder vessels.

Car carriers

Ferries and RO/RO ships.
